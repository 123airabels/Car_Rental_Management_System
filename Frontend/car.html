Before you book

  • You need a verified driver's license that is still valid on the last day of your rental. Upload it from My profile after you register so staff can verify it.
  • Your request is reviewed by our staff. The car is yours once it is approved.
  • The total shown is an estimate: the daily rate times the number of days.
  • Returning late adds a late fee, charged at your booked daily rate for each started 24 hours after a short grace period.
  • You can change the dates or cancel a rental from My rentals while it is still pending or approved. Changing the dates of an approved rental sends it back to staff for approval.
